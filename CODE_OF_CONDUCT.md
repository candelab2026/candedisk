# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want taking part in CandeDisk to be a respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, appearance, disability, ethnicity, gender, identity, experience, nationality, religion or orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and respectful, even when you disagree.
- Accept constructive criticism, and give it constructively.
- Focus on what is best for the project and the people who use it.
- Assume good faith in others.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Insults, personal attacks, trolling or discriminatory comments.
- Harassment, public or private.
- Publishing other people's private information (addresses, personal data) without permission.
- Any other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

The maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and contributions that violate this code and, in serious cases, temporarily or permanently exclude whoever violates it.

To report unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainers through the GitHub profile [candelab2026](https://github.com/candelab2026). All reports are handled confidentially.

This text is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
